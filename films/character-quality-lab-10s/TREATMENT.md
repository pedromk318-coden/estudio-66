# Character Quality Lab — 10-second vertical proof
## Visual target
A richly illustrated 2D cutout character in a warm, intimate bar stage. This is a quality test, not a finished episode. The singer has a designed silhouette, layered curls, readable facial features, a tailored jacket with fabric texture, metal jewelry and a hand-held microphone. The set uses separate depth planes, practical lighting, soft background focus, warm volumetric spill, dust and a moving camera.
## Beat
- 0.0–1.0s: establish the stage and character already in a held pose.
- 1.0–4.8s: singer raises into the phrase; mouth, head, shoulders and microphone move independently.
- 4.8–5.2s: a quick visual accent and camera snap.
- 5.2–8.8s: performance continues; warm particles drift through the key light.
- 8.8–10.0s: she lands the final phrase and holds.
## Technical target
1080×1920, 24 fps, 10 seconds, deterministic frame function, Chromium/Canvas-compatible SVG layers, two render workers, MP4 H.264 via the existing pipeline. This proof is designed to expose character-design quality before scaling to 1–3 minute episodes.
## Important limitation
The character is authored vector artwork, not an AI-generated painted illustration. The test validates a more detailed layered illustration/rig pipeline; it does not claim a full skeletal animation editor or GPU acceleration.
